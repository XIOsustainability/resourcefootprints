Resource footprints

Environmental and socio-economic footprints of 44 countries and 5 rest-of-world regions, 1995-2024, calculated with EXIOBASE 3.11.3.

  • Footprint explorer: production-based, consumption-based and import-embodied results for 22 indicators, by region and by sector group.
  • Data coverage: observed and projected years of each account in EXIOBASE 3.8.2 to 3.12.

Notes

  • Dotted lines are projected years.
  • Sector groups (biomass, fossil resources, metals, non-metallic minerals, remaining economy) follow UNEP IRP, Global Resources Outlook 2024, Methodological Annex 2, Table A2.1. Impacts are assigned to the sector where they occur.
  • The row GWP100 - AR6 - total non combustion in EXIOBASE 3.11.2 and 3.11.3 omits SF6, HFC, PFC and NF3. Non-combustion GHG is shown here as total minus combustion.
  • Group values are sums of member regions. Group imports include trade between members.
