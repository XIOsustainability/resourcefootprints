Notes on the data

  • Results use EXIOBASE 3.11.3, whose release is expected on 30 October 2026. They can change before then. Years after the last observed year of each account are projected and are drawn dotted in the charts.
  • Resource groups follow UNEP IRP, Global Resources Outlook 2024, Methodological Annex 2, Table A2.1. Impacts are counted in the sector where they occur, so electricity used to process materials counts under the remaining economy. The Global Resources Outlook attributes more of the supply chain to extraction and processing, and its shares for greenhouse gases are higher.
  • In EXIOBASE 3.11.2 and 3.11.3 the characterised row GWP100 - AR6 - total non combustion leaves out SF6, HFC, PFC and NF3, about 2% of global greenhouse gases; the total and combustion rows are complete. This site derives non-combustion emissions as the total minus combustion.
  • Country groups are sums of their member regions. For a group, imports embodied are the sum of member imports and include trade between members.
